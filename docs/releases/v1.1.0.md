## GitHub release notes (draft)

### Live Traffic Routing 1.1.0

**Routing**
- **Traffic forecast** ("Spread drivers over detours"): roads that many drivers are about to drive look busier before
  they jam, so traffic spreads over several detours instead of all piling onto one.
- **Keep through traffic out of neighbourhoods**: local streets that suddenly get much more traffic than usual look
  slower to drivers passing through. (Local streets are now recognised correctly; see Fixes.)
- **Smarter re-routing**: drivers re-route at the next junction where there is a real choice, also when they are stuck
  in a jam, and never from inside a junction. The re-route limit follows how busy the game's route planner is.
- **Emergency vehicles** on an emergency run re-route around jams.
- **Auto preset**: tunes itself to your city, one small tested step at a time.
- **Temporary roadworks** (statistics panel): close a road, or ask drivers to avoid or prefer it, to test your network
  without deleting anything. Not saved; cleared when you load a save.

**Bottleneck finder** (statistics panel, Causes tab)
- Finds where each queue actually starts, not where the cars stand, and why: traffic lights, turning across oncoming
  traffic, give way or stop signs, busy junctions, lanes merging, cars merging onto a highway, people crossing (at a
  crosswalk or not), buses or service vehicles stopping in the lane, parking, cars entering from buildings.
- Shows it on the map: the queue (with its direction), the junction, the turns the queued cars want, and long arcs to
  where they come from and where they go. Street names in the details move the camera there.
- Lane and junction advice: which lane can do what (from the left, as the driver sees it), too few lanes for the main
  turn, shared turn lanes, turn lanes that are too short, merges before or after the junction, queues that reach back
  into the previous junction, queues of mostly trucks.
- Fix ideas: a new road, a missing turn or a place to turn around that would let the stuck drivers skip the queue.
- Ranked over the last 24 game hours, only problems that keep coming back; each one says whether it is getting better
  or worse, and "cleared" once a fix worked.

**Statistics**
- How people travel: car share of travellers, people walking, cycling, on public transport, waiting for a route. A
  stronger setting can make people leave the car; this shows it.
- Vehicles the game removes when they are stuck or have no route, per hour and where.
- Traffic news, forecast and re-route details, Copy summary in the Compare tab, coloured status badges.

**Fixes**
- Local streets are recognised correctly: the game stores road speed limits at about twice the shown value, so before
  this almost no road counted as a local street (the "On local streets" metric and the neighbourhood setting).
- Cyclists are no longer counted as car passengers or removed cars.
- The previous session's log is kept as `Logs/LiveTrafficRouting-prev.log` (the game rewrites log files on start).

**Good to know**
- Nothing is stored in your save. The bottleneck finder only runs while the statistics panel is on and changes no
  routing.
- Statistics files gain new columns; older files are upgraded automatically (with a backup). `bottlenecks.csv` and
  `links.csv` from test builds are renamed to `.old.csv`.
- Local-street numbers recorded with 1.0 are not comparable with 1.1.
